How to play

Grandma Juniper left you her farm in Golden Hour Valley. The village's Great Lantern is fading — grow and gather everything on its offering list to light it again.

  • Till farm grass with the hoe, plant seeds, and water them every day. Watered crops grow overnight.
  • Ship anything by holding it and using the shipping bin beside your house. You're paid while you sleep.
  • Buy seeds, food and upgrades at Mabel's Stall in the village (8 AM – 8 PM).
  • Using tools costs energy. Eat food or go to bed. Stay up past 2 AM and you'll pass out and lose some gold.
  • Glowcaps grow wild south of the river. Sunshowers water your crops for you.
